Красочное описание

Authentic Roman recipe made with crispy guanciale, aged pecorino cheese, fresh egg yolks, and plenty of black pepper. Creamy without cream, exactly how it should be.

menu_book
Состав блюда

Ингридиенты:

  • Spaghetti
  • Guanciale
  • Egg Yolk
  • Pecorino Romano Cheese
  • Black Pepper
  • Salt

Приготовление:

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
  2. While pasta cooks, crisp guanciale in a pan until golden. Remove guanciale, leaving rendered fat.
  3. In a bowl, whisk egg yolks with grated Pecorino Romano and plenty of black pepper. Add a splash of pasta water.
  4. Drain pasta, reserving pasta water. Add pasta to the pan with guanciale fat.
  5. Quickly add the egg mixture and a bit more pasta water, stirring vigorously to create a creamy sauce. Add guanciale back in.
  6. Serve immediately, garnished with extra Pecorino and black pepper.
